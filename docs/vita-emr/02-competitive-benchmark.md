## The three things Epic does that are genuinely hard to replicate

1. **Care Everywhere network effects.** Epic's cross-institution
   record-sharing value compounds with every additional Epic hospital —
   a new entrant's interoperability, even if technically superior
   (FHIR-native, open APIs), starts with zero network density.
   **Strategy: route around.** VITA's "acid test" export (Section 8) plus
   a genuinely open, standards-based national/regional HIE adapter (Section
   8) trades network lock-in for regulator- and payer-mandated interop —
   in a market where a national HIE is being built anyway (the Philippine
   HIE ambition under the UHC Act, for the illustrative deployment market
   in `01-clarification-gate.md`), VITA can plug into that shared
   infrastructure instead of trying to out-network Epic bilaterally.
2. **Revenue-cycle depth.** Epic's RCM (Resolute) reflects decades of
   payer-specific edge cases, denial patterns, and contract-modeling logic.
   **Strategy: partner, then match narrowly.** Section 5's Layer 4 (agentic
   coding/denials/prior-auth) is explicitly designed so a new payer is a
   config change, and the initial build partners with a claims
   clearinghouse (per `01-clarification-gate.md` #9) rather than
   re-deriving payer-specific logic from scratch. Depth is matched
   incrementally, starting with the illustrative market's actual payer mix
   (PhilHealth + a bounded set of HMOs), not attempted globally on day one.
3. **Forty years of accumulated clinical content** (order sets, care
   pathways, documentation templates, calculated CDS logic tuned across
   thousands of go-lives). **Strategy: route around + match where it
   matters most.** Doctrine #6 in the build prompt ("order sets... authorable
   by clinicians without engineering") converts this from a vendor content
   library into a per-site authoring capability, seeded with a smaller
   curated starter library (aligned to WHO/DOH standard treatment
   guidelines for the illustrative market) rather than attempting to
   out-author Epic's library before launch.
